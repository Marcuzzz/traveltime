TravelTime

Automatically records how long you spend travelling — by car, train, bus, bike or on foot — and lets you share your log as CSV.

Install for Android All releases

Requirements

  • Android 8.0 (API 26) or newer, with Google Play services
  • You'll need to allow installs from unknown sources — see below.

First-time install

Since this is side-loaded (not from Google Play), Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser or file manager the first time. Open Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps and enable it for the app you're downloading with.

Tap the button above on your Android device, then open the downloaded TravelTime-release.apk.

After installing

  • Open the app and tap Turn on tracking. Allow Physical activity (and notifications, for the running timer).
  • Set the app's battery usage to Unrestricted if trips are missed (Settings → Apps → TravelTime → Battery).
  • Share your trips via the share icon: This week, This month or All trips as a CSV file to WhatsApp, email, Drive, …

Privacy

No GPS, no account, no servers. Trips are stored only on your phone and leave it only when you share them.
